# App guide

## Pages
Home gives an overview of the project. The Dams page lists every reservoir with its location, the regions affected if it overflows, and the previous recorded level. The Predictions page is the forecasting workbench.

## Predictions page controls
- Reservoir picker: search and choose a dam; the chart, details panel and water display follow the selection.
- Forecast horizon: choose 1, 7, 14 or 30 days ahead.
- History window: choose how many past days the chart shows, from 120 to 365 days.
- Show predicted level: draws a dashed line for the forecast level on the water display.
- As-of date: pick any earlier date to see what the forecast looked like then. Buttons step one day back or forward, Latest jumps back to the newest date, and Play steps forward automatically (speed adjustable). Keyboard left and right arrows also step.
- Reset session clears the running error statistics.

## Reading the chart
The grey line is observed levels, the dashed cyan line is the forecast, purple points are the actual observed values after the as-of date, and the shaded band is the uncertainty range. The red zone marks levels above the full reservoir level (FRL).
