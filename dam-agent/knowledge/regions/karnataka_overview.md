# Karnataka regions

## Coverage
The app currently covers reservoirs in Karnataka.

## Rivers and basins
The 10 major reservoirs span 4 major hydrological drainage systems:
- **Krishna Basin**:
  - Almatti Dam (Krishna River, Vijayapura / Bagalkote).
  - Bhadra Dam (Bhadra River, tributary to Tungabhadra, Chikkamagaluru / Shivamogga).
  - Malaprabha Dam (Malaprabha River, tributary to Krishna, Belagavi / Bagalkote).
  - Tungabhadra Dam (Tungabhadra River, main Krishna tributary, Vijayanagara / Koppal / Ballari).
  - Vani Vilasa Sagara (Vedavathi River, sub-tributary to Tungabhadra, Chitradurga).
- **Cauvery Basin**:
  - Krishna Raja Sagara / KRS Dam (Cauvery River, Mandya / Mysuru).
  - Kabini Dam (Kabini River, tributary to Cauvery, Mysuru / Chamarajanagar).
  - Hemavathy Dam (Hemavathi River, tributary to Cauvery, Hassan / Mandya).
- **Sharavathi Basin (West-Flowing)**:
  - Linganamakki Dam (Sharavathi River, Shivamogga / Uttara Kannada).
- **Kali Basin (West-Flowing)**:
  - Supa Dam (Kali River, Uttara Kannada).

## Districts and flood-prone downstream areas
- **Almatti Downstream**: Bagalkote, Vijayapura, Raichur, and Yadgir districts along the Krishna river corridor. High spillway releases also impact the confluence zones in northern Karnataka.
- **Tungabhadra Downstream**: Vijayanagara, Koppal, Ballari, and Raichur districts; downstream riverbeds in Andhra Pradesh (Kurnool).
- **Bhadra Downstream**: Bhadravathi town, Shivamogga city, and low-lying agricultural plains along the confluence with Tunga at Kudli.
- **KRS Downstream**: Srirangapatna island, Mandya taluks, Mysuru rural belt, and downstream reaches towards T. Narasipura and Tamil Nadu border.
- **Kabini Downstream**: Nanjangud temple town, Mysuru district riverbanks, and confluence with Cauvery.
- **Hemavathy Downstream**: Holenarasipura taluk, Hassan rural areas, and Mandya agricultural canals.
- **Malaprabha Downstream**: Saundatti, Ramdurg, and low-lying agricultural tracts of Bagalkote.
- **Sharavathi (Linganamakki)**: Jog Falls gorge, Gerusoppa, and Honnavar coastal estuary.
- **Kali (Supa)**: Dandeli wildlife reserve river banks, Ganeshgudi, Kadra, and Karwar coastal confluence.

## Monsoon and hydrograph pattern
- **Southwest Monsoon (June – September)**: Western Ghats catchments (Sharavathi, Kali, Upper Cauvery, Bhadra) receive intense orographic rainfall (often exceeding 3,000–5,000 mm annually). Inflows peak dramatically between July and August, rapidly refilling reservoir live capacities.
- **Northeast / Retreating Monsoon (October – November)**: Brings cyclonic showers to south and eastern dry zones (Chitradurga, Mandya, Vijayanagara).
- **Depletion Season (December – May)**: Reservoirs steadily draw down storage to supply drinking water (notably Bengaluru from KRS and Kabini), rabi irrigation, and hydroelectric power generation.
