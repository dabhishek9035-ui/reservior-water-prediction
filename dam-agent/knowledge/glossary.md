# Glossary

## FRL
Full Reservoir Level: the highest level to which the reservoir is designed to be filled under normal operation.

## MDDL / minimum level
Minimum Draw Down Level: the lowest level from which water can normally be drawn. In this app the minimum or dead-pool level marks the bottom of the active range.

## Active range and percent full
The active range is the span between the minimum level and FRL. "Percent full" in the app is the level's position inside that range, not the percent of total volume.

## Gross, live and dead storage
Gross storage is the total capacity up to FRL. Dead storage is the water below the lowest outlet that cannot be drawn. Live storage is the usable part between them.

## TMC
Thousand million cubic feet, a unit used for reservoir storage in India.

## Catchment area
The land area whose rainfall drains into the reservoir, in square kilometres.

## As-of date
The date from which a forecast is made. Only observations up to the as-of date are used.

## MAE and RMSE
Mean absolute error is the average size of the forecast error. RMSE is the square root of the average squared error and punishes large misses more.

## Persistence
A baseline forecast that assumes the level stays at its last observed value.
